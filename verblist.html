🔸 Why is 来る irregular?

来る (kuru) is one of only two truly irregular verbs in Japanese (along with する). Unlike godan or ichidan verbs, its stem changes unpredictably across conjugations:

Dictionary: 来る (kuru)
ます form: 来ます (kimasu)
て form: 来て (kite)
Negative: 来ない (konai)

Notice how the reading changes: ku-ru → ki-masu → ki-te → ko-nai. This vowel shift (u → i → i → o) doesn't follow any standard pattern, which is why it must be memorized separately.
